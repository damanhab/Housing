## 2. What's left that is clean

| Rank | Name | Meaning | Screen |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **بيتنا — Baytna** | "Our home" | ✅ No Saudi company found; baytna.sa, bytna.sa and baytna.com.sa free. ⚠️ Looks like **Bayt.com** (Gulf job site used by HR, our buyer) and **Bayut** (property portal); the logo and spelling must keep it distinct. |
| 2 | **مبنى — Mabna** | "Building" | ✅ No Saudi company found; mabna.sa and mabna.com.sa free. ⚠️ Functional and cold; sounds a bit like Mabaat (short-stay proptech) and Mabanee (Kuwaiti developer). |

### Expert view
- **Baytna is the warmest and most direct.** "Our home" is collective: it speaks to a crew living together, which "Mnzil" (a home) doesn't. It's easy in English ("Bayt-na"), and every Arabic speaker and most Urdu speakers get it instantly. Taglines: «بيتنا… بيت طاقمك» / *"Baytna: home for your crew."*
- **Mabna** is honest about what we run (whole buildings) and sounds investor-grade, but it has no emotion.
- **The trade-off to accept:** with Mnzil holding "home", any pure housing word puts us head-to-head on meaning. Baytna stands apart through the word *"our"* (community), which ties back to the brand idea.

## 3. Before deciding

Ministry of Commerce trade-name reservation · SAIP trademark search (classes 36, 37, 42, 43), specifically checking against Bayt / Bayut · secure baytna.sa / bytna.sa / baytna.com.sa plus social handles · test with HR managers, landlords and workers.
