## Footprint that overlaps ours

Heavily **Eastern Province**, which is where SakanHub launches:
- Khobar: Karim Hotel, The Address Residence, Marriott Residence, Wave Resort, Kingdom Gate, Waraf, Al-Raka and Al-Hamra compounds, **Terrace View 1 and 2 (economy)**, **Luxury Hospitality Residence (economy, 89 rooms)**, Dyafa Inn Khobar (pipeline).
- Dammam: Wyndham Garden, Karim Dammam, **Al Shiraa Residence (375 units)**, **Terrace View 3 (economy, 147 rooms)**, two pipeline hotels.
- Also Ras Tanura, Abqaiq, Riyadh, Makkah, Madinah, Hail, Al-Ahsa, Unaizah, Al Majmaah.

There is **no worker or staff housing anywhere in the profile.** Dyafa is a hospitality company.

## How Dyafa intersects with SakanHub and Mnzil

In short:
1. **Customer:** they employ 600+ staff plus 300+ O&M manpower, with ~1,440 rooms opening in 2026–27. That workforce needs licensed group housing, much of it in Khobar/Dammam.
2. **Supplier or partner:** catering (ISO 22000/HACCP), O&M manpower, procurement, the Fandaqah PMS, and Ejar brokerage are all things SakanHub would otherwise buy or build.
3. **Supply of buildings:** their economy residences in Khobar/Dammam are the kind of stock that could be licensed as group housing if hotel-style occupancy is weak.
4. **Potential competitor:** they already have every ingredient (operator licence, O&M, catering, Ejar, Eastern Province base, municipal compliance record) to launch worker housing. Mnzil's SAR 1bn fund makes the category visible; an operator like Dyafa could follow.
5. **Mnzil:** no direct overlap today. Dyafa could be an O&M or catering contractor for Mnzil's fund compounds, or compete with Mnzil's "Operate My Unit" for landlords.
