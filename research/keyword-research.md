## What it means

- **«سكن عمال» belongs on the home and city pages,** not an article. At about 6,600 searches a month, it is the main commercial search.
- **The licence cluster is the biggest informational opportunity** (~1,450 a month in Arabic, plus 210 in English).
- **The proof certificate has almost no search volume.** It becomes a section inside the licence and renting articles instead of an article of its own.
- **Two other topics have no reportable volume:** building conversion and price searches.
- **English:** "labour camp" and "staff accommodation" are what people type, not "group housing".

## Mnzil's SEO (mnzil.com, checked 18 Oct 2026)
