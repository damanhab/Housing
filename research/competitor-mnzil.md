## The October 2026 move — SAR 1bn fund

On ~5 October 2026 Mnzil, **The Development Group (TDG)** and **Capital Partners** announced a **SAR 1bn ($267m) real-estate fund** to build purpose-built compounds for **up to 50,000 workers** in Riyadh, Jeddah, Dammam, Khobar, Makkah and Madinah. Development starts Q4 2026.
- Mnzil = strategic partner and **exclusive operator**
- TDG = site selection, design, development
- Capital Partners = fund manager

Implied capex ≈ SAR 20,000 per worker of capacity — far below the ~SAR 55,750/bed SARCC/Mawref benchmark in our background research. Either land is excluded, the fund is levered beyond SAR 1bn equity, or the product is much lighter. **Worth digging into — it tells us their real cost assumptions.**

## Business model (three layers at once)

1. **Sublease** — master-lease buildings, fit out, resell beds (the bulk of current revenue, most likely).
2. **C2B marketplace** — landlords list worker housing, companies book (Airbnb-style).
3. **Purpose-built** — now scaled up through the SAR 1bn fund.

Plus add-ons: transport, catering, "Operate My Unit" (managing landlords' buildings), Early Wage Access; remittance and health insurance planned.

## Reading of their strategy

- **Strengths:** first-mover brand, top-tier US VC, blue-chip logos, multi-city footprint, institutional capital partner, and they now own the "tech-enabled worker housing" story with media.
- **Direction:** moving **up-market and capital-heavy** — large purpose-built compounds, enterprise clients, fund-backed development. This is a multi-year build cycle (first fund assets likely 2027–28).
- **Possible weaknesses / openings:**
  - **Spread thin:** marketplace + sublease + development + fintech + transport + catering, across 30 cities, in under 3 years. Commentators already flag a lack of focus.
  - **Segment:** they serve SMEs and even individual beds, so "they ignore SMEs" is **not** a valid gap. The fund's long-term-lease strategy does point new capacity at large anchor clients.
  - **Exclusive-operator lock:** being tied to one fund's assets may make them less neutral as an operator for other landlords and developers.
  - **Compliance as a side feature:** their pitch is cost + convenience. The buyer's real fear is **CR suspension** through the Balady–Qiwa link (see `background/regulation-block-a.md` §2).
