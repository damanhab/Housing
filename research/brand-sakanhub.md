# Brand Platform — SakanHub (سكن هب)

**Status:** Lead candidate (founder pick, 6 Oct 2026). Not yet reserved.
**Last updated:** 6 October 2026 (visual identity finalised)

## 1. Why SakanHub works

- **"Sakan" (سكن)** is the market's own word (سكن العمال، السكن الجماعي). Zero explanation needed, as direct as "Mnzil".
- **"Hub"** says *platform*: one place where companies find, book and manage housing for their crews, and where building owners fill their beds.
- It fits the founder's taste: descriptive and modern like Foodics or Hungerstation, with an Arabic core like Mnzil.
- Easy in Arabic, English and Urdu. No ح ع خ غ ق.

## 2. Strategic implication (decision needed)

The name **pulls the strategy toward a platform**. Mnzil is now operator-first (exclusive operator of its SAR 1bn fund's compounds). SakanHub can own the opposite position:

> **Mnzil = the operator (like a hotel chain). SakanHub = the hub for every licensed worker bed in the Kingdom (like a booking platform).**

- **Companies:** search, compare, book and manage beds across many buildings and operators, in one dashboard (contracts, invoices, occupancy, and licence status in the background).
- **Building owners:** list, get licensed, fill and get paid (this connects to supply strategy B).
- **Our own operated buildings:** still exist as "SakanHub Places", but they're one part of the supply, not the whole story.

Honest note: Mnzil also has a C2B marketplace, but its brand and capital now point to operating its own assets. Being the **neutral hub** is a difference we can actually own. This is a strategy choice, not just branding; to be confirmed by the founder.

## 5. Name treatment

- **Latin:** `SakanHub` (one word, capital H). Owns search results better than "Sakan Hub".
- **Arabic:** `سكن هب`. Saudi startups usually transliterate "hub" as «هب».
  - ⚠️ On its own, «هب» can read as «هَبّ» ("blew") or «هَبْ» ("give").
  - The logo must make it read as a single brand word.
  - Test «سكن هب» vs «سكن هاب» with 10 Saudi HR managers.
- **Product architecture:**
  - **SakanHub for Business** (companies)
  - **SakanHub Owners** (landlords)
  - **SakanHub Places** (our operated buildings, e.g. "SakanHub Al Sulay")
  - **SakanHub Verified ✓** (licensed-bed badge)

## 8. Risks

1. **Descriptiveness (trademark).** "سكن" + "hub" for housing services may be judged descriptive by SAIP. Mitigation: file it as a **combined mark** (stylised logo plus words) in Arabic and Latin, classes 36, 43, 42 and 9. Get an IP lawyer's opinion before spending on the brand.
2. **Sakan.co** (a Kuwaiti bilingual property platform serving GCC buyers, including Saudi Arabia) and the government's **Sakani** programme. Both are similar in sound, but different products. A distinct logo and the "Hub" suffix carry the separation.
3. **"Hub" is common in startup names** (many companies use "Hub"). That's acceptable because the Sakan + Hub pairing is unique, but brand recall must come from the logo and colour.
