## 0. Terminology

The official regulatory term is **السكن الجماعي للأفراد** — "group housing for individuals." The market term *سكن العمال* ("labor housing") does not appear in the governing instruments.

This matters practically: searching the market term returns SEO blog content, much of which carries outdated or incorrect figures. Searching the official term returns the ministry instruments. All correspondence with authorities should use the official term.

## 1. Authorities and Instruments

### 1.1 Primary instrument

**الشروط الصحية والفنية والسلامة اللازم توافرها في المساكن الجماعية للأفراد**
(Health, Technical and Safety Conditions Required in Group Housing for Individuals)

Issued by the Ministry of Municipalities and Housing (MOMAH / وزارة البلديات والإسكان).

Version history, as far as it can be established from public sources:

| Version | Evidence |
|---|---|
| Ministerial Decision 4500763245/1, 1445H / 2024 | PDF hosted on MOMAH site |
| Umm Al-Qura gazette publication, 8 August 2025 | Cited as primary reference by licensed operators |
| Updated version, covered in trade press 1 September 2026 | Grants one year to correct; cancels conflicting prior decisions |

The updated version takes effect from publication in the official gazette, grants licensed group housing up to one year to correct their operational position, and exempts buildings and complexes previously licensed as group or private housing from the spatial-planning requirements.

Document structure (8 chapters): definitions → classification → licensing requirements → spatial planning requirements → technical requirements → fire prevention and protection → operational requirements → general provisions, grievances and complaints.

### 1.2 Governance body

**البرنامج الوطني لتطوير السكن الجماعي للأفراد** (National Program for Developing Group Housing for Individuals), chaired by MOMAH.

Member entities:
- Ministry of Interior
- Ministry of Health
- Ministry of Human Resources and Social Development (MHRSD)
- Ministry of Commerce
- Ministry of Industry and Mineral Resources
- Royal Commission for Jubail and Yanbu
- Saudi Authority for Industrial Cities and Technology Zones (MODON)
- Economic Cities and Special Zones Authority (ECZA)
- Public Health Authority (Weqaya)

### 1.3 Three separate authorities, three separate proofs

Satisfying one does not discharge the others:

1. **MOMAH / the amanah or municipality** — municipal licensing and permitted-use classification
2. **Civil Defense** — fire safety approval, a precondition to the operating licence
3. **MHRSD** — the employer's obligations toward the worker

### 1.4 Labour Law overlay

The February 2025 Labour Law amendments made accommodation a statutory obligation: employers must either provide accommodation and transportation or pay a cash allowance in lieu. Previously this was market custom rather than statute.

## 2. The Threshold and the Enforcement Mechanism

**This is the single most commercially significant section of Block A.**

- Group housing licensing is **mandatory for establishments with 20 or more workers**.
- Obligation is triggered by headcount and housing type — **not** by job title.
- All licences issue through the **Balady** platform; some licences inside industrial cities issue from the competent authorities.
- Capacity for each property is determined by the **area of the building or complex**.
- Establishments must hold licensed housing **proportionate to the workforce registered on Qiwa**.
- **A housing allowance is not an exemption.** Paying workers a monthly housing allowance does not release the establishment from the group-housing licence requirement. Source: Al-Watan, 29 Dec 2025: «صرف بدل سكن شهري للعمالة في القطاع الخاص، لا يعفي المنشآت من شرط الحصول على رخصة سكن جماعي».

### 2.1 Enforcement

Electronic linkage between **Balady** and **Qiwa** enables automated compliance verification. The consequences come in two layers:
- **Qiwa services suspended:** no visa-quota expansion and no worker transfers (Okaz / SPA, 17–18 Sep 2025). This bites first and constantly.
- **Commercial registration suspended** where there is no valid group-housing licence (MOMAH official page: «يتم إيقاف السجل التجاري للمنشأة في حال عدم وجود ترخيص ساري للسكن الجماعي»).

Field inspection tours are also running (Okaz, Sep 2025).

The obligation covers the **entire entity and all its branches**, including cases where commercial registrations are unified under a single number.

### 2.2 Phasing

The enforcement phase began **September 2025**, applied gradually:
1. Large establishments
2. Medium establishments
3. Small establishments

> **Commercial implication:** CR suspension, not the fine schedule, is what drives demand. The fines are trivial relative to the cost of compliance (see §5.3). The business risk being sold against is operational shutdown, and the buyer is a compliance officer or HR director, not a procurement clerk optimising bed price.

## 3. Physical Standards

### 3.1 Core numbers

| Item | Requirement |
|---|---|
| Floor area per person, bedroom | **4 m² minimum** |
| Maximum occupants per room | **10 persons** |
| Sanitary set (WC + handbasin + shower) | **1 per 8 persons or fewer** |
| Common rest / dining room | **≥ 0.7 m² per person** |
| Windows for natural light | **≥ 8%** of room floor area, half of which must open for ventilation |
| Bathroom window | 0.30 m² |
| Capacity > 1,000 persons | First-aid room with nurse licensed by the Saudi Commission for Health Specialties |
| Capacity > 5,000 persons | Clinic with licensed doctor and nurse, plus occupational health and safety services |
| Licence duration | **1 year**, renewable |

### 3.2 Mandatory provisions

- Central kitchen or catering service
- Equipped isolation room for infectious disease cases
- Security cameras per the approved system and its implementing regulation
- **Full-time Saudi supervisor** responsible for the housing, with access to all facilities and full knowledge of emergency procedures
- Complete separation of men's and women's accommodation via independent entrances and facilities
- Bed, bedding and a lockable wardrobe per person
- Contracted specialist cleaning and disinfection company, or dedicated staff, with a daily cleaning log

### 3.3 Prohibitions

- Cooking stoves or washing machines inside bedrooms
- Housing individuals on roofs, in corridors, or in basements
- Asbestos in mobile cabin construction
- External balcony projections within setbacks, or AC units installed in them

### 3.4 Mobile cabins (Category C)

- Use limited to project duration or one year maximum, renewable
- Bedroom ceiling height **≥ 2.2 m**
- Cabin floor raised **20 cm** above surrounding ground level (flood mitigation)

### 3.5 Utilities

- Safe drinking water; clean, rust-resistant tanks, **disinfected every 6 months**
- Upper tanks fully insulated, capacity for **≥ 5 days**, kept **≥ 2 m** from the sewage pit
- Ground-floor drainage connected directly to the main line, separated from upper-floor runs
- Electrical wiring concealed within walls
- Heating and cooling systems with air-purification filters in bedrooms
- Condensate collection recommended where cooling load exceeds 350 tons, for irrigation reuse

### 3.6 Fire safety

- Civil Defense approval required **before** the operating licence issues
- Approved evacuation plan
- Fire extinguishers spaced no more than **23 m** apart
- Illuminated emergency signage operating for **90 minutes** on power failure

### 3.7 Capacity is calculated, not counted

Capacity derives from bedroom area, facility counts and shared spaces — **not** from the number of beds that physically fit. A 24 m² net bedroom licenses **6 persons**, not 10.

> Common failure mode: a licence is valid, but headcount has grown past approved capacity. Relocation requires a new licence; it is not fixed by amending the lease.

### 3.8 ⚠️ Correction to widely-circulated figures

Several high-ranking Arabic guides state figures that **do not match the current regulation**:

| Circulating (incorrect) | Actual |
|---|---|
| Max 8 persons per room | **10** |
| 1 WC per 6 workers | **1 sanitary set per 8** |
| Ceiling height 2.7 m | Not the stated figure; 2.2 m applies to mobile cabins |
| Windows 10% of floor area | **8%**, half openable |
| Fines SAR 10,000–100,000 | **SAR 600–5,000** (see §5.3) |

Do not cite these sources in any deliverable.

## 5. Third-Party Operator Track

### 5.1 Who holds the licence

Responsibility falls on the **usufruct holder (مالك حق الانتفاع)**:

- **Building or complex owner** — where units are leased to multiple tenants using them as group housing
- **Lessee** — where the entire building or complex is leased
- **Project executor** — for mobile cabins inside a project

The usufruct holder **may contract a specialised and authorised establishment** to manage and operate the group housing. This is the legal basis for a third-party operating model.

### 5.2 Two documents — and the commercial opening sits between them

| Document | Held by | Proves |
|---|---|---|
| **رخصة سكن جماعي**<br>Group housing licence | Owner or whole-building lessee | The property itself is licensed and compliant within its approved capacity |
| **شهادة إثبات سكن جماعي**<br>Housing proof certificate | Tenant establishment renting units inside an already-licensed property | The lease relationship and that qualifying housing exists for its workforce inside the licensed property |

A lease alone proves nothing. The property must be licensed first; the tenant then issues a proof certificate against the licence holder's approval, which requires the valid licence number, lease details, and room and WC counts and areas.

**A separate independent licence cannot be issued for part of a building.** The applicant must comply with one of the three approved categories in full. This constrains any strategy based on converting a single floor or wing.

### 5.3 Penalties

| Violation | Fine (SAR) |
|---|---|
| Using the licence for other than its designated purpose (e.g. simultaneous family and individual housing); failing the per-person bedroom area; failing minimum sanitary provision (1 WC + handbasin + shower per 8 or fewer); exceeding licensed capacity | 1,000 – 5,000 |
| No equipped isolation room; no electricity source; no potable water; no catering service, central kitchen, or minimum kitchens per floor in complex buildings; kitchens not properly equipped | 600 – 3,000 |
| Installing certain barriers | 400 – 2,000 |

The updated penalty schedule applies graduated fines by violation type, repetition, city economic tier, and establishment size per Monsha'at classification, with a warning applied before penalty in defined cases.

**The fines are not the deterrent. CR suspension is.**

### 5.4 Application documents (Balady)

- Valid commercial registration with the activity matching the applicant's track
- Site coordinates
- Property type, built area, room count and areas, WC count
- Title deed (if owned) or lease contract (if leased)
- Recent clear photographs
- Building completion certificate (buildings and complexes)
- Project owner's approval (mobile cabins inside a site)
- Civil Defense safety permit or approval

### 5.5 Process

1. Log in to Balady via National Single Sign-On
2. Select applicant capacity, then the group housing licence service
3. Enter location, property, ownership or lease, and capacity data
4. Upload documents and submit
5. Municipality verifies spatial compliance → initial approval or return with notes
6. **90 days** from initial approval to complete remaining requirements
7. Technical and safety inspection via municipality or engineering office
8. Fees paid → licence issues electronically

### 5.6 Grievances

| Dispute type | Forum |
|---|---|
| Construction licence disputes | Amanah / municipality committees |
| Penalties issued by field inspection committees | **Board of Grievances (ديوان المظالم)** |
| Saudi Building Code violations | Documented by the entities competent to apply the Code |

## 6. Zoning and Location

### 6.1 Permitted locations

- Sites designated for **commercial or commercial-residential use**, on a commercial street approved by the amanah or municipality with its width registered on Balady, with the **site entrance on that commercial street**
- Within the boundaries of **workshop, warehouse, light industrial and car showroom** plans — as a fully and independently planned separate site at a safe distance from hazardous areas
- Within **category (A) and (B) fuel station** facilities, per approved service centre requirements
- In **agricultural areas**, as a component of an agricultural project, for that project's own workers
- Within a **hospital**, as one of its facilities

### 6.2 Prohibited or constrained

- Not on roads, streets, or vital / tourist-classified areas defined on Balady by the amanah or municipality
- **≥ 300 m** from schools and hospitals within the urban boundary
- Site entrance on a road **≥ 20 m wide**
- Concentration of individuals in particular districts and neighbourhoods to be avoided
- Must be consistent with approved land uses
- Away from workplaces emitting air pollutants (gases, dust, smoke, liquid and solid waste)
- Not in hazardous areas, flood channels, antiquities zones, or environmentally significant areas such as nature reserves
- Privacy of neighbouring buildings to be respected; no visual blight

### 6.3 Parking and landscaping

- **1 car parking space per 40 persons** (buildings and complexes inside the urban boundary)
- **Bus parking for half the resident count**
- Accessible parking near main entrances
- **1 tree per 100 m²** of unbuilt open area

### 6.4 ⚠️ MODON constraint — directly relevant to Eastern Province siting

MODON industrial land is designated **solely for establishing and operating factories** — not warehouses and not worker housing. Worker housing is classified as an **investment activity (نشاط استثماري)**. Partners must apply for **investment or residential land** through the **Shareek (شريك)** system.

MODON also operates its own group housing licence service through Shareek, separate from the Balady track.

**Consequence:** industrial plot pricing cannot be used as a land-cost assumption for a bed-count model. This must be corrected before any Block C capex-per-bed figure is built.

## 7. Caveats and Verification Required

1. **The MOMAH PDF body text did not extract.** Chapter structure was obtained directly from the ministry PDF; clause-level detail was reconstructed from MOMAH's own announcements and Arabic trade-press coverage of the gazetted text, which are mutually consistent. **Before any bid or investment commitment, the gazetted Arabic PDF must be read end to end.**

2. **Version ambiguity.** At least three versions exist (2024 ministerial decision, August 2025 gazette publication, September 2026 update). Every figure cited externally should carry its version and date.

3. **Spatial rules defer to the local amanah.** Dammam and Khobar municipalities define their own prohibited streets and zones on Balady. Site-level confirmation is a **Balady inquiry**, not a document read. Prior licensing experience in another city does not transfer.

4. **Industrial city sites follow a different track entirely** — MODON or the Royal Commission for Jubail and Yanbu, not Balady.

5. **Housing allowance:** settled. An allowance does **not** exempt the establishment (see §2). Workers living with their families remain unclear and should be confirmed with the authorities.
