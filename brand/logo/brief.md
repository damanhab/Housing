# Design brief — SakanHub (سكن هب)

**Date:** 6 October 2026 (logo wizard, round 2)

- **Summary:** A Saudi platform where companies find, book and manage housing for their workforce in one place.
- **Audience:**
  - Primary: HR and operations leads, and investors.
  - Secondary: building owners and workers.
  - First touchpoints: the **website and investor deck**. It must still work as a favicon.
- **Brand adjectives:** modern, techy, solid, trustworthy.
- **Avoid feeling:** cold, generic, charity, clip-art.
- **Big idea:** home + hub + community: many homes, one platform.
- **Admired:**
  - **Airbnb:** a simple symbol with a meaning.
  - **Roshn:** Saudi architectural cue, premium.
  - **Tabby / Noon:** simple, colour-led.

## Lessons from round 1 (rejected)

- An H monogram reads like **HungerStation**. No H marks.
- A single letter isn't indicative enough. The symbol must say *housing*.
- Complex marks (the س with teeth, the 3×3 grid) fail as favicons. **The 16px test is mandatory.**

## Category clichés to avoid (or reinvent)

Generic house outline with a chimney, key, skyline, door, roof floating over text, generic blue or green, location pins.

## Directions for round 2 (3 different mark types)

- **A — Symbol + name:** a pictorial/abstract symbol combining home, hub and community.
- **B — Architectural (Roshn cue):** a Saudi/Najdi architectural element made into a mark.
- **C — Symbol built into the name:** the name carries the housing idea itself.

## Other decisions

- **Must work on:** website header, investor deck, social avatar, favicon (16px), app icon; later signage and buses.
- **Colour:** the designer decides. Show greyscale first, with a colour hint for the recommended direction. Avoid the generic Saudi green and proptech blue.
- **Language:** bilingual lockup, with no preferred lead.
- **Lettering:** open. Test lowercase vs camel case.
