What we heard

The brief

A Saudi platform where companies find, book and manage housing for their workforce in one place. It should feel modern, techy, solid and trustworthy, and say 'housing' at a glance without clip-art.

moderntechysolidtrustworthy

Success: says housing instantly, integrates with the name, readable at 16 px, works in one colour, bilingual (Latin + Arabic), avoids generic Saudi green and proptech blue.

SakanHub — identity concepts2
Concept A

The n is a home

The n in sakan becomes a home, with the hub (a dot) at its door: the name is the logo.

  • Built on the Noto Sans Bold grid: same stem, roof apex on the ascender line, inner roof on the x-height
  • Clay accent (#C8502A) owns a warm, Najdi-earth colour in a category of greens and blues
  • Bilingual lockups with Noto Kufi Arabic; dot-free favicon cut for 16 px
