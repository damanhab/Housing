# Surface brief — Home (AR primary, EN mirror)

**Scope:** `site/src/pages/ar/index.astro` + `site/src/pages/en/index.astro` (shared components). **Visitor mode:** Persuade.

**Audience:** company HR / ops lead (primary); building owners secondary.
**Job:** get licensed worker housing in a city, fast.
**Action:** submit a bed request (city, workers, start date, phone), or WhatsApp.
**Proof available:** regulation facts only; no clients, numbers or prices. **Constraints:** a service, not listings; no paragraphs; bilingual RTL/LTR; test-site mode.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The category standard at Stripe / Careem / Tabby craft. A service landing page whose first viewport *is* the request. It refuses listings and browsing, and refuses the AI-template tells: gradient blobs, icon tiles above headings, cream plus serif, rows of three equal cards.

OWN-WORLD:
- White and sand (`#F6F3EE`) grounds, ink (`#1D2433`) type, hairline borders.
- Clay (`#C8502A`) only on the primary action, the house icon and active states.
- Nunito (Latin) and Almarai (Arabic): rounded, firm weights. Generous whitespace, precise 8-pt grid, crisp rounded-12 panels.

STORY: The visitor understands that one request gets licensed beds, transport and catering in their city. They believe it because the page shows the licensing rules applied to *their* numbers. They act by sending the request or opening WhatsApp.

FIRST VIEWPORT:
- Nav: logo, Companies, Building owners, Cities, Regulations, language switch, WhatsApp.
- Start side: H1 «سكن عمّالك… بطلب واحد» with a one-line support.
- End side, at equal scale: the working request panel (city chips, worker count, start date, phone, primary clay button) fused with a live "what your request needs" readout (rooms at max 10 per room, sanitary sets at 1 per 8, licensing track Balady vs RCJY/MODON).
- A compliance trust line under it.

FORM: Canon (the category standard), chosen by the founder over the dealt hand; seed key acc16863. Signature interaction: the request readout updates as the visitor types (rule-derived, labelled as an estimate). Motion grammar: numbers settle in under 200 ms, no scroll theatre.

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
